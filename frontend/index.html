About Medical AI

Medical AI Helper is a tool that allows you to upload medical documents and ask questions about them in simple, understandable language.

It extracts key information such as diagnoses, symptoms, medications, lab results, and follow‑up instructions — always with page citations so you can verify the source.

This tool is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.
